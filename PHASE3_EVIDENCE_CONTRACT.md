# ATLAS Phase 3 Paired Evidence Contract

Champion is the canonical Production decision and remains authoritative. For the first Challenger hypothesis, eligible canonical WAIT observations are paired with the captured candidate direction as a research-only shadow action.

## Eligibility
- Epoch: `HTF_SR_V2_2026-09-14`
- Regime: `4H_DIRECTIONAL_12H_NEUTRAL`
- Primary blocker family: `HTF_CONFLICT`
- Captured direction: LONG or SHORT

## Evidence
Measure 4H, 8H and 12H directional return, positive-return rate, 12H MFE/MAE, invalidation count and missed-opportunity count. Always report LONG and SHORT separately as well as aggregate evidence.

## Guardrails
- Canonical action remains WAIT.
- Shadow action is research-only and cannot override Production.
- Threshold remains 68.
- No score, risk, SL/TP or execution mutation.
- At least 30 matured 12H samples only makes the cohort eligible for review; it never authorizes promotion.
- Legacy and post-V2 evidence must not be mixed for current-version claims.
- Promotion requires a separate evidence review and a new Production epoch if approved.
